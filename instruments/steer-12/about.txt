steer-12 sequencer

Performance sequencer with potentiometer for writing CV values, and 12-pad array for creating rhythms.
- the rhythm array is configured in 1/4, 1/8, and 1/16 note steps.

Button controls are:
- 1 RECORD: holding this button and moving potentiometer will write data values into sequencer. 
    - holding this button and touching pads will turn steps ON
- 1 & 2 ERASE: holding this button and touching pads will turn steps OFF
- 2 RATCHET: repeats the curret note. potentiometer will change divisions
- 1&2&3 RECALL: hold and press a pad to recall a saved pattern
- 3 DAMP: damps momentarily. hold 1&3 to set pads to DAMP. Potentiometer will set damp amount
- 4 CV: record a secondary CV pattern
- 1&2&3&4 SAVE:  hold and press a pad to save the current pattern to that pad

LEDS:
- 1: Flashes on beat 1
- 2: Flashes when event is triggered. Dims acording to damp
- 3: Flashes when a damped note is triggered
- 4: shows current value of CV

Pads:
- press one pad to reset sequence to that step
- press 2 pads to set reset sequece and set last step
- press more than two pads to make a momentary loop

Input:
12 capsense inputs from MPR121
4 buttons
4 leds
1 potentiometer

Output:
MIDI via USB
MIDI via 1/8" TRS
